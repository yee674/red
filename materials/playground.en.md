**Core identity**: The campus exercise ground with horizontal bars and a sand pit — one of the places where Mao trained his body.

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- Several wooden horizontal bars stand beside the courtyard, their surfaces polished by use, above a sand floor.
- The bars are set at different heights for students of different sizes, with simple equipment for jumping and climbing nearby.
- Together with the sports-field rostrum and the cold-water well, they form on-site evidence of the school's emphasis on physical education.

**Key facts**

- Mao took cold-water, wind and rain baths, and regularly climbed, swam and worked out on the bars.
- In "A Study of Physical Education" he argued for "civilising the spirit and toughening the body".
