**Core identity**: The Dongfanghong campus of Hunan First Normal University, one of its main campuses.

**Campus spaces**

- Located on Fenglin Road, Yuelu District, Changsha, centred on the library, Runzhi Square and Na Lake.
- Main study stops: Tian Han Theatre, Art Museum, Library, Runzhi Square, Celebrity Wall.
- Together with the Chengnan campus (the old school site), it forms the "thousand-year academy, century-old normal school" landscape.
