**Core identity**: The Art Museum on the Dongfanghong campus, a space for aesthetic education and school-history-themed exhibitions.

**What to see**

- The museum stands near the art building, by Cherry Blossom Avenue and Heming South Building.
- Exhibitions feature staff and student art and design, and school-history-themed works; schedules follow on-site notices.
- A good place to discuss how art tells school history.
