**Field notes (31 Oct 2026)**

- The well stands on higher ground, fenced with wood and explained by a bilingual "The Well" sign.
- The sign records that Mao took cold-water baths here throughout his years at the school, believing they "train vigour and fearlessness".
- Below the well are a stone retaining wall and a lawn — a quiet place to stop and explain.
