**Core identity**: The library on the Dongfanghong campus, the document centre for teaching and research.

**What to see**

- The library stands in the north-central campus, beside Runzhi Square and Na Lake, and is a campus landmark.
- Collections focus on teacher education, Huxiang culture, red documents and basic-education research.
- The terrace overlooks Runzhi Square and Na Lake — a good spot to discuss campus space.
