**Core identity**: A society founded in 1918 by Mao Zedong, Cai Hesen, Xiao Zisheng and others, and a core force in Hunan's anti-imperialist and anti-feudal movement.

**Key facts**

- Its oldest member was He Shuheng, and Xiao Zisheng was its first secretary-general.
- Most of its members were 'students of Mr Yang Huaizhong', and its founding was closely tied to Yang Changji's educational influence.
