**Core identity**: Mao's 1950 inscription for his alma mater: "To be a teacher of the people, first be a student of the people."

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- The inscription is enlarged in Mao's calligraphy, gold on a dark ground, on the school-history exhibition wall.
- A small caption notes that Mao wrote it for Hunan First Normal School in 1950.
- It hangs beside the school-spirit plaque "Seek truth from facts, do not presume yourself right"; together they form two main threads of the school's spirit.

**Key facts**

- In December 1950, at the school's request, Mao wrote the inscription.
- The sentence expresses both an expectation for teacher education and his enduring view of the people and of learning.
