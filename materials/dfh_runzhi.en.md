**Core identity**: Runzhi Square on the Dongfanghong campus, named after Mao Zedong's courtesy name Runzhi.

**What to see**

- The square lies south of the library on the west bank of Na Lake, a core landscape and gathering space.
- "Runzhi" was Mao's courtesy name, linking a historical figure directly to campus space.
- With the library and Na Lake it forms the campus axis and a natural meeting point.

**Links in the knowledge graph**

- Located in: Dongfanghong Campus
- Commemorates: Mao Zedong

**Study prompts**

- Find a spot where you can see both the library and Na Lake, and photograph the campus axis.
