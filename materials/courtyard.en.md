**Core identity**: The campus courtyard, centred on the sculpture group "Nurtured by great teachers, sharpened by good friends".

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- A large tree anchors the courtyard, surrounded by bronze figures of students and teachers, standing or seated, recreating scenes of debate and study.
- A central boulder is inscribed with eight characters: "Nurtured by great teachers, sharpened by good friends" — the key to the school's influence on the young Mao.
- With grey walls, green windows and covered corridors around it, this is the most atmospheric public space on campus.

**Key facts**

- The school brought together teachers such as Yang Changji, Xu Teli and Li Jinxi, and classmates such as Cai Hesen, He Shuheng and Xiao Zisheng.
- "Great teachers and good friends" sums up the two threads in Mao's youth.
