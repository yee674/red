**Field notes (31 Oct 2026)**

- Several wooden horizontal bars survive beside the courtyard, above a sand floor polished by use.
- Together with the sports-field rostrum and the well, they are on-site evidence of the school's physical education.
- In "A Study of Physical Education" Mao placed body before moral and intellectual education: the body is "the vehicle of knowledge and the lodging of morality".
