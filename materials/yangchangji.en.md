**Core identity**: Teacher of ethics and self-cultivation at Hunan First Normal School, the teacher who impressed Mao Zedong most, and later his father-in-law.

**Key facts**

- From 1914 to 1918, while Mao studied at the school, Yang Changji was his teacher of self-cultivation.
- On first meeting Mao he wrote approvingly, 'Such fine natural gifts are rare indeed'; Mao's essay 'The Power of the Mind' received full marks and high praise from him.
- The founding of the New People's Study Society was closely bound up with Yang's educational influence: its members were, in his words, 'mostly students of Mr Yang Huaizhong'.
- In June 1918 Yang wrote from Beijing to members of the society with news of the work-study programme in France, which directly prompted Mao and others to travel to Beijing.
