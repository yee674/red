**Core identity**: Mao Zedong Statue Square on the Chengnan campus, with a bronze statue of the young Mao at its centre.

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- The statue shows the young Mao; the pedestal is inscribed with his name and dates, and a stone stele behind introduces the square.
- The open square is the main meeting point for study groups, wreath-laying, group photos and pre-visit briefings.
- At dawn and dusk the statue's silhouette contrasts with the distant city skyline — a good spot for an opening photo.

**Key facts**

- Mao studied at the school from 1913 to 1918 and later taught there; the school was central to the formation of his early thought.
- The statue square is the core commemorative space of today's campus, used for ceremonies, teaching and public visits.
