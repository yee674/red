**Core identity**: The disarming of warlord troops at Houzishi, a student military action near the school in November 1917.

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- A large diorama recreates the scene: hills, retreating soldiers, armed students and captured weapons.
- Nearby boards explain the event and how the school's student volunteer corps was organised.
- The terrain and costumes are reconstructed from historical sources and can be compared with the archives.

**Key facts**

- In November 1917, during the Constitutional Protection War, retreating Beiyang troops passed Houzishi in southern Changsha, threatening the city.
- Mao organised the school's student volunteers, joined by police, and used firecrackers and lights to create the impression of a large force, forcing the troops to surrender their weapons.
- The action shows the young Mao's organisational ability and courage, and marks the beginning of his early military practice.
