**Field notes (31 Oct 2026)**

- A cultural wall on the west side, titled "Chengnan Campus", introduces the academy's thousand-year history and the founding of the school.
- Stone inscriptions and line drawings, with steps and planting in front, make it a good first lesson in school history.
- Walking north from the wall leads to the Chengnan Academy archway and the north gate.
