**Core identity**: The Tian Han Theatre on the Dongfanghong campus, named after alumnus Tian Han.

**What to see**

- The theatre stands in the south-west of campus, near Tian Han Road and the Heming North Building (music building), and is a main venue for performances and lectures.
- Tian Han was an alumnus, a founder of modern Chinese drama and the lyricist of the National Anthem.
- The façade and entrance make a good stop for an "art and school history" theme.
