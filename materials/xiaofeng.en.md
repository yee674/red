**Core identity**: The school spirit of Hunan First Normal University — "seek truth from facts, do not presume yourself right".

**Origin and meaning**

- In December 1950 Xu Teli inscribed these words for his alma mater and proposed them as the school spirit.
- "Seek truth from facts": start from reality and study the internal relations and laws of things.
- "Do not presume yourself right": do not proceed purely from subjective assumptions; accept criticism and reflect on oneself.
- The school motto is Mao's inscription, "To be a teacher of the people, first be a student of the people." The motto concerns whom one serves and from whom one learns; the spirit concerns how one studies and behaves.
