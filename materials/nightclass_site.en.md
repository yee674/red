**Core identity**: The site of the Night School for Workers on the Chengnan campus, with its door plaque and exhibition boards.

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- The rammed-earth wall carries a vertical wooden plaque reading "Night School for Workers", with a red interpretation board beside the door.
- Exhibition boards use old photographs and text to introduce enrolment, courses and Mao's teaching.
- The room beyond the door is small — imagine workers crowding in after a day's work to learn to read.

**Key facts**

- In 1917 Mao founded the Night School for Workers at the school, teaching nearby workers free of charge and writing his own lecture notes.
- The night school was an important early step in Mao's practice of social education and brought him into direct contact with workers.
