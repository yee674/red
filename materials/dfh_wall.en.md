**Core identity**: The Celebrity Wall on the Dongfanghong campus, a display of school figures and campus memory.

**What to see**

- The wall stands in the south of campus near Dongfanghong Square, linking school history through portraits and panels.
- It features historic teachers, distinguished alumni and revolutionary figures, and can be compared with entity cards in the graph.
- A good closing stop for a "school figures" route.

**Study prompts**
