**Core identity**: The stone archway at the entrance of the Chengnan campus, inscribed "Chengnan Academy".

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- A three-bay, four-pillar stone archway with cloud-and-dragon reliefs; the lintel reads "Chengnan Academy" from right to left.
- Couplets are carved on the pillars; though weathered, phrases about the academy's scholarly lineage can still be made out.
- Just inside the archway are the north gate and the visitor desk — the first stop of a study visit.

**Key facts**

- The school's predecessor, Chengnan Academy, was founded by Zhang Shi in the Southern Song; the archway is the physical marker of that thousand-year lineage.
- In 1903 the academy became the Hunan Normal College, later developing into Hunan First Normal School.
