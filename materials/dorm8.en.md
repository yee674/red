**Core identity**: The dormitory of Class Eight on the Chengnan campus, restored with the original long beds, desks and lamps.

**What you can see (field photo, 31 Oct 2026)**

- The room has a long communal bed; Mao's place was by the window, marked by a small sign reading "Mao Zedong's bed".
- Simple desks and stools stand beside the beds; students attended class by day and studied here at night.
- An exhibition board records Mao's "three no-talks" in the dormitory: no talk of money, no family trivia, no gossip about men and women — so that classmates could focus on study.

**Key facts**

- Mao lived on campus; the dormitory was where he read, talked late into the night and discussed current affairs with classmates.
- The "three no-talks" show his self-discipline and concentration, and influenced many classmates.
